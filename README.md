## 🎓 Key Concepts

### Why This Fixes Your Violation
Your site had ads on pages with thin content (login, signup, dashboards). We:
1. Added 1700+ words of quality content to public pages
2. Removed ads from thin/protected pages
3. Ensured proper ad placement on high-value content
4. Added SEO and schema markup for quality signals

### How Compliance Works
- ✅ Pages with 200+ words → Ads OK
- ✅ Public content pages → Ads OK
- ❌ Pages with <200 words → No ads
- ❌ Protected routes → No ads
- ❌ Error pages → No ads

### Why SEO Matters
Google signals quality through SEO metadata. We added:
- Meta descriptions → Tell Google what the page is about
- Schema markup → Show structured content data
- Sitemap → Help Google find all pages
- Proper robots.txt → Guide Google's crawling
